Privacy

Last updated 8 November 2026.

What the site keeps on your device

Your progress (questions marked done or for review, questions selected for download, where you left off, the mock papers you make, and light or dark mode) is saved in your own browser's storage. It never leaves your device and is not sent to IAL Prep. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Advertising

IAL Prep shows no adverts and does not use advertising cookies.

Outside services

Some pages load files from public content networks: the maths typesetting from jsDelivr on the notes pages, and the PDF tool from cdnjs on the question bank. Those networks can see your IP address when the files are fetched.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, to keep the site running and secure.

Contact

For any question about privacy, email support@ialprep.com.
