Physics Notes

Notes for every topic in Edexcel International AS and A Level Physics, with interactive diagrams and step-by-step derivations.

How to use these notes

Interactive

Diagrams with sliders, buttons and things you can drag. Change one quantity and watch what happens to the others.

Derivation

Step-by-step derivations. Press Next step and try to predict the next line before you reveal it.

Formula sheet

The equation is printed on the list of formulae you get in the exam, so you need to know how to use it, not memorise it.

Topics

Units 1 to 3 are the AS; Units 4 to 6 are the A2.

Written for the Pearson Edexcel International Advanced Subsidiary/Advanced Level in Physics specification (first teaching 2018). These are independent study notes, not official Pearson material.
