  • Deceleration at given position from v(x) WME03 June 2026 Q1(a) 4 marks

    Particle moving along the positive x-axis away from O with velocity v given as a function of x (v = 6/(x^2 + 4) per the scheme); find the deceleration at x = 3, showing all stages (calculator-only solutions not accepted).

    Use algebraic integration (all stages shown) to show v = pt + q ln(2t + 3) + r ln 3, with p, q, r to be found, given acceleration as a function of t and v = 2 ln 3 at t = 0.
